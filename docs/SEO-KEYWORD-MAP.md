## Notes & limits

- **Database**: Semrush `us` only. TechD's market is US Fortune 500, so this is correct. If regional expansion happens later, re-run for `uk` / `ca`.
- **Volume reality check**: Most B2B IBM-product terms show low volume in Semrush. That's normal — the buyers exist, they just don't generate huge keyword volume. Win on **specificity** (product + role + industry), not on broad reach.
- **Quota**: ~30 Semrush calls consumed during this phase. No quota errors hit.
- **Source-of-truth**: every Volume / KDI / SERP figure above came from a Semrush call logged in this loop. Where the cell says "(low)" or "(no data)", Semrush returned no measurable volume — the page should be written for the human reader and pick up long-tail incidentally.
